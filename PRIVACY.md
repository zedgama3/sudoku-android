# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-25_

This policy covers the Sudoku app for Android published by Jared Fowkes.

## Short version

The app collects no personal data, and it never sends anything off your device.

## Details

- **No data collection.** The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or
  tracking of any kind. It contains no third-party SDKs that collect data.
- **No network access.** The app doesn't request the Internet permission, so it can't send or
  receive data.
- **What's stored on your device.** Your current game, pre-generated puzzles, statistics and
  settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They stay there until you
  clear the app's data or uninstall it.
- **Device backup.** If you've turned on Android's backup, Android may include this app's data
  in your device backup to your Google account. Google handles that backup under its own
  privacy policy. The developer never has access to it.
- **Links.** The About screen links to the app's source code and license on GitHub and to the
  developer's email address. Tapping one opens your browser or email app, and what you do
  there is covered by that service's privacy policy.
- **Children.** The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this policy will be updated first, along with the
date above. The full history is visible in the
[source repository](https://github.com/zedgama3/sudoku-android/commits/main/PRIVACY.md).
